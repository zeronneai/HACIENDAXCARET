The Invitation

"A different kind
of retreat."

Not a resort.
A return.

Hacienda Presencia is not a resort. It is a small, deliberate program for guests who arrive overstimulated and depart restored — held by a centuries-old hacienda tucked into the jungle of Playa del Carmen.

The Setting

A private estate in the
heart of the Riviera Maya.

9 Suites Privadas
18 Máximo Huéspedes
4 Días & Noches

A private, gated colonial estate inside the Hacienda at Xcaret — palms, stonework, a natural cenote-style pool. Walking distance to Río Secreto, ten minutes from the Caribbean coast.

The Itinerary

The Rhythm of Presence.

i

Arrival & Grounding

  • Private transfers from Cancún or Playa del Carmen
  • Welcome circle with ceremonial cacao
  • Restorative yoga — opening the body after travel
  • Welcome dinner under the palms
  • Evening sound bath — crystal bowls and gong
ii

Earth, Body & Ceremony

  • Sunrise yoga in the jungle pavilion
  • Longevity workshop — sleep, metabolic health, recovery
  • Excursion to Río Secreto — underground cenote river
  • Guided breathwork session
  • Temazcal Ceremony — Mayan sweat lodge led by a curandero
iii

The Sea Day

  • Sunrise stand-up paddle on the Caribbean
  • Private catamaran — open water sailing
  • Reef swim and free time at sea
  • Sunset dinner poolside
iv

Integration & Departure

  • Closing yoga — gentle movement and stillness
  • Farewell breakfast at the hacienda table
  • Memory workshop — attention and recall frameworks
  • Closing circle — intentions carried home
  • Private transfers to the airport
