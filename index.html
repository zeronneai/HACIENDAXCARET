The Program

Eight modalities. One arc.

i

Temazcal

A circular stone chamber, medicinal herbs, and therapeutic steam. The Maya temazcal purifies the body and activates the immune system through moist heat and aromatic vapor. Guided by a certified practitioner.

ii

Sound Healing

Crystal singing bowls, gong, and layered voice. Vibratory frequencies reduce cortisol, modulate the autonomic nervous system, and produce deep rest, in under twenty minutes.

iii

Breathwork

Conscious breathwork activates the parasympathetic nervous system, releases accumulated somatic tension, and restores the capacity for self-regulation. The most direct tool for changing how you feel, in seconds.

iv

Yoga

Daily practice at dawn in the open pavilion above the jungle. Gentle Vinyasa to awaken the body, Yin for deep tissue and accumulated tension, Nidra for active rest. Adapted to all levels.

v

Memory Workshop

Learn the attention, memory, and focus frameworks used by the world's top executives, athletes, and artists. Neuroscience-validated techniques you can implement from day one back home.

vi

Longevity Workshop

Practical protocols for high-quality sleep, metabolic health, active recovery, and longevity markers. Based on research from Huberman, Attia, and Sinclair. Translate them into concrete routines from day one.

vii

Stand-Up Paddle

Quiet paddles at dawn on the turquoise Caribbean. Paddling is meditation in motion: balance, breath, and total presence with the water and morning silence before the world wakes up.

viii

Catamaran

A full day of private sailing aboard a luxury catamaran. Coral reefs, snorkeling, ceviche on board, and the Caribbean horizon as the only limit. Adventure as a practice of total presence.
